01 — Welcome

What is
Page One?

A six-week pilot accelerator by Machine Cinema for creative AI projects. We take a cohort of creators from concept to a pitchable proof-of-concept — culminating in a Demo Day in front of funders, studios, and distributors.

02 — How It Worked

How it worked

📍

In-Person

Tuesdays, 6–9 PM at Digital Garage, San Francisco. Hands-on building, demos, and live feedback.

💻

Online

Thursdays, 6–7:30 PM. Tool walkthroughs, guest talks, and check-ins between studio sessions.

🎬

One Project Each

Every creator picked one project and committed. Six weeks to a screenable proof-of-concept.

🎤

Demo Day

Week 7. A YC-style showcase — 5-minute pitch, screening, and Q&A in front of the industry.

6 weeks 9 projects 2 sessions / week 1 Demo Day

02 — How It Worked

The seven-week arc

WeekThemeFocus
WK 1 Foundation & Ideation Intro day — program philosophy, business & legal context, project selection.
WK 2 Development Kickoff Tool demos, AI creative workflows, building visual worlds and narrative.
WK 3 Pitch Prep Pitch structure, storytelling in 5 minutes, early feedback rounds.
WK 4 Refinement Story polish, advanced AI workflows, 1-on-1 check-ins.
WK 5 Final Polish Full pitch rehearsals, peer feedback, production quality review.
WK 6 Final Rehearsal Tech check, run-throughs, last mentor feedback, final tweaks.
WK 7 Demo Day Present to funders, producers, distributors, and industry leaders.

04 — What Changed

The ground moved
under us

Building with AI means the toolset shifts mid-sprint. It did — repeatedly — during Cohort 01.

🌊

Seedance Dropped

A major new video model released in the middle of the program. Creators rebuilt workflows mid-cohort to take advantage of it.

⚡

Weekly Releases

New models, features, and pricing landed almost every week. What you pitch on Demo Day may not exist on day one.

🧭

Tool-Agnostic by Design

We taught judgment, not a single pipeline — so the cohort could adapt instead of betting on one tool.

04 — What Changed

What we learned

Cohort 01 was a pilot — and it taught us a lot. Six things we're carrying into the next cohorts.

🎯

Clearer Weekly Objectives

Every week should open with a concrete, written goal.

📌

Milestone Check-Ins

Regular checkpoints to keep every project on track.

🗓️

Structured Tuesdays

In-person sessions need a clear agenda and arc.

🎤

Demo Day Clarity, Earlier

Set expectations for Demo Day from week one — not week five.

⭐

Define "Success"

Give creators a clear picture of what a strong outcome looks like.

📍

Intentional In-Person Time

Use the room for what only the room can do.
