What is
Page One?

A six-week pilot accelerator by Machine Cinema for creative AI projects. We help 17 creators go from concept to a pitchable proof-of-concept, culminating in a Demo Day in front of funders, studios, and distributors.

02 — The Program

Four pillars

🎯

The Goal

Each participant is building a creative AI project — episodic series, feature films, interactive experiences, transmedia worlds, visual narratives. They leave with a pitch package and a proof-of-concept they can screen.

👥

The Cohort

17 creators from diverse backgrounds — filmmakers, writers, designers, musicians, technologists, producers. Skill levels with AI range from beginner to advanced.

📍

The Format

Tuesdays in-person, 6–9 PM at Digital Garage, SF. Thursdays online, 6–7:30 PM. Mentors can join either or both.

🎤

Demo Day

Week 7. YC-style showcase — 5-minute pitch, screen proof-of-concept, Q&A. Audience: film funders, studios, streaming platforms, distributors.

04 — Expectations

What we're asking of mentors

01 / Share what you know

Our participants want perspective from people who've been there. Share honestly.

02 / Be direct & honest

Specificity is generosity. The cohort opted into a culture of low-ego feedback.

03 / Be available

One session, one creator, one pitch deck review — all of it helps.

04 / Respect the process

Participants own their projects and IP. Mentorship is guidance, not direction.

05 / Keep it confidential

Project ideas, pitch materials, and creative work shared in the program are confidential.

05 — Schedule

Program timeline

WeekThemeFocus
WK 1 Foundation & Ideation Introductions, project selection, goal-setting, legal/business context.
WK 2 Development Kickoff Tool demos, AI creative workflows, building visual worlds and narrative structure.
WK 3 Pitch Prep Pitch structure, storytelling in 5 minutes, early pitch feedback rounds.
WK 4 Refinement Story polish, advanced AI workflows, 1-on-1 check-ins.

WK 6 Final Rehearsal Tech check, run-throughs, last mentor feedback, final tweaks.
WK 7 Demo Day Present to funders, producers, distributors, and industry leaders.
06 — Get Involved

Ways to participate

🎙️

Give a Talk

Lead a 20–45 minute session on something you know deeply. Tuesday in-person or Thursday online.

👥

Mentor a Group

Work with 2–4 participants who share a common challenge. Once or twice over the program.

🔍

1-on-1 Reviews

Review a creator's pitch, concept, or proof-of-concept. A single 30–60 minute conversation can unlock a breakthrough.

🌐

Demo Day & Beyond

Attend Demo Day as audience, panelist, or connector. Bring funders, distributors, and producers into the room.
