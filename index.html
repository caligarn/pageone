01 — Welcome

What is Page One?

A six-week pilot accelerator by Machine Cinema for creative AI projects. We help 17 creators go from concept to a pitchable proof-of-concept, culminating in a Demo Day in front of funders, studios, and distributors.

02 — The Program

Four pillars

🎯

The Goal

Each participant is building a creative AI project — episodic series, feature films, interactive experiences, transmedia worlds, visual narratives. They leave with a pitch package and a proof-of-concept they can screen.

👥

The Cohort

17 creators from diverse backgrounds — filmmakers, writers, designers, musicians, technologists, producers. Skill levels with AI tools range from beginner to advanced.

📍

The Format

Tuesdays in-person, 6–9 PM at Digital Garage, SF. Thursdays online, 6–7:30 PM. Mentors can join either or both — whatever works.

🎤

Demo Day

Week 7. YC-style showcase — 5-minute pitch, screen proof-of-concept, Q&A. Audience includes film funders, studios, streaming platforms, distributors.

04 — Expectations

What we're asking
of mentors

Share what you know

Our participants are looking for perspective from people who've been there. Share honestly.

Give direct, honest feedback

Specificity is generosity. The cohort has opted into a culture of direct, low-ego feedback.

Be available when you can

One session, one creator, one pitch deck review — all of it helps. Tell us what works.

Respect the creative process

Participants own their projects and IP. Mentorship means guidance, not direction.

Keep it confidential

Project ideas, pitch materials, and creative work shared in the program are confidential.

Program timeline

06 — Get Involved

Ways to participate

🎙️

Give a Talk

Lead a 20–45 minute session on something you know deeply. Tuesday in-person or Thursday online.

👥

Mentor a Group

Work with 2–4 participants who share a common challenge or medium. Once or twice over the program.

🔍

1-on-1 Reviews

Review a creator's pitch, concept, or proof-of-concept. A single 30–60 minute conversation can unlock a breakthrough.

🌐

Demo Day & Beyond

Attend Demo Day as audience, panelist, or connector. Bring funders, distributors, and producers into the room.
